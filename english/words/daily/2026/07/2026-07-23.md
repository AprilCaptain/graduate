### 代表性训练句

1. The teacher failed to convey the idea clearly to unfamiliar students.
2. It struck her that the data might be incomplete.
3. Several organizations formed a coalition to oppose the plan.
4. Greater transparency can increase public trust in government decisions.
5. The government must coordinate its response with local communities.
6. Excessive fear can paralyse effective decision-making.
7. Higher income tends to correlate with better access to education.
8. Advances in neurology have improved our understanding of the brain.

### 薄弱词与易错点

- `convey`：`fail to convey` 是“未能传达”；`clearly` 修饰“传达”，不是修饰 `idea`。
- `paralyse`：是“使……瘫痪、使……无法进行”，不能直接译为“丧失”。
- `coalition`：`organizations` 是“组织”，`oppose the plan` 是“反对计划”。
- `coordinate`：注意 `coordinate A with B`；`response` 在句中指“应对措施”。
- `correlate`：`higher income` 是“更高的收入”，`tends to` 表示“往往、倾向于”。
- 周边词需结合句法确认：`fear` 是“恐惧”，`critics` 是“评论家”，`situation` 是“情况、局势”。

### 句法问题

- 注意副词修饰关系：`convey the idea clearly` 是“清楚地传达这个想法”。
- 注意介词结构：`trust in...` 表示“对……的信任”，`access to education` 表示“受教育机会”。
- 注意固定结构：`It struck someone that...` 表示“某人突然想到……”。
